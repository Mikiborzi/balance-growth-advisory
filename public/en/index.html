Our Services

We don't describe solutions.
We build them with you.

Five service areas in logical sequence:

01 · Organisation

Organisational & Process Optimisation

Balance & Growth Advisory analyses and redesigns organisational and management processes to build structures capable of sustaining growth without losing control. Before seeking external resources, the business must work well from within.

  • Process analysis and redesign
  • Scalable organisational structures
  • Internal governance and control systems
02 · Governance

Financial Governance

We build advanced management control systems that allow you to read the real numbers, anticipate financial pressures and make decisions on solid data — not on perception, not on instinct: on clarity.

  • Advanced management control systems
  • Integrated financial planning
  • KPI dashboards for timely decision-making
03 · Leadership

Operational Leadership

As a Fractional CFO or CEO, Balance & Growth Advisory provides stable senior leadership during the most critical phases: accelerated growth, restructuring, transition and market expansion. Senior expertise inside your business, without the constraints of a full-time hire.

  • Fractional CFO / Outsourced financial direction
  • Interim general management
  • Growth, restructuring and transition oversight
04 · Partners

Strategic & Financial Partners

The value of a network is measured by the quality of its nodes. Balance & Growth Advisory identifies, evaluates and structures alliances with industrial, commercial and financial partners — in Italy and international markets. Not just who you know: the right partner for you, at the right moment.

  • Strategic partner identification and selection
  • International development and access to new markets
  • Joint ventures, commercial alliances and cross-border structures
05 · Finance

Ordinary & Extraordinary Finance

We identify, evaluate and manage the full spectrum of available financial instruments: from ordinary credit to incentivised finance, from M&A transactions to capital increases. Every instrument at the right moment, with the right structure.

  • Credit access and banking relationship management
  • Grants, incentives and public funding
  • Extraordinary transactions: M&A, mergers, capital increases
Who We Work With

Two business moments,
one approach: from structure to value.

In Expansion

Companies in an accelerated growth phase

Growth without structure consumes resources without generating value. We build the governance system that allows you to scale in a controlled way, identifying finance and partnership opportunities at the right moment.

In Transition

Companies in restructuring or reorganisation

Phases of discontinuity require clarity, method and fast decisions. We build the structure to emerge with greater solidity than before — not to survive, but to restart stronger.

In both cases, Balance & Growth Advisory doesn't operate from a distance. We enter the business, analyse the real situation, build the tools and oversee execution. Not an external observer: an operational partner who shares the route and walks it alongside you.

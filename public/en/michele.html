Profile

Systems that work, organisations that grow.

Michele Borzatta is partner at Balance & Growth Advisory for the organisation and compliance area. Project Manager and Business Partner with over twenty years of experience in building and governing quality management systems and Organisational Management and Control Models (in line with international compliance frameworks including ISO 37001 and anti-bribery standards).

He has operated as an entrepreneur and manager in complex contexts — social cooperatives, consortia and national business networks — in the Healthcare, Social Welfare, Training and Active Labour Policy sectors. Direct experience in the organisational governance of multi-site structures involving hundreds of people.

Co-founder of Starting Work Srl Impresa Sociale and Mestieri Lombardia, a network of over 20 Employment Agencies accredited by the Lombardy Region and authorised by the Italian Ministry of Labour. Member of the Board of Directors of the Fondazione Alessandro Volta di Como.
